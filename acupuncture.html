What's involved

Needling as part of your wider plan

We use acupuncture and dry needling alongside - not instead of - physiotherapy assessment and treatment.

  • Targeted needling. Fine, sterile, single-use needles placed at specific points or trigger points.
  • Pain relief support. Used to help ease pain and muscle tension so you can move and exercise more comfortably.
  • Combined with physiotherapy. Always alongside a wider assessment and treatment plan, not as a stand-alone treatment.
  • Discussed before treatment. We'll explain what to expect and answer any questions before your first session.
Who it's for

Where needling can help

Acupuncture and dry needling can be a useful addition for a range of musculoskeletal complaints.

  • Muscle tension & trigger points. Tight, knotted muscles that haven't responded to other treatment alone.
  • Chronic aches. Long-standing musculoskeletal pain alongside your wider treatment plan.
  • Sports & overuse injuries. Used alongside rehab to help manage pain during recovery.
  • Anyone curious to try it. If you're not sure it's for you, we're happy to talk it through at your appointment.
First appointment

What to expect at your first appointment

Every plan starts with understanding you - how you move, work and live - before we agree what's next.

  • One-on-one care. A full 45 minute initial appointment.
  • Evidence-based. Treatment grounded in current research, not guesswork.
  • A plan you'll follow. Simple home exercises and lifestyle advice.
Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Does acupuncture hurt?

Most people feel little more than a brief pinprick, if anything at all. We'll always check you're comfortable throughout.

Is it safe?

Yes — we use sterile, single-use needles and follow recognised safety guidelines. We'll always check your medical history first.

How many needles are used?

This varies by treatment area, but is usually a small number of needles placed precisely rather than many at once.

Can I have acupuncture on its own, without other physiotherapy?

It's offered as part of a wider physiotherapy assessment and plan, so we can make sure it's the right fit for what's going on.
