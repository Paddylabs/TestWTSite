Why Wellness Therapeutics?

Recovery that treats you, not just the symptom

We take a holistic view of your health and wellbeing - how you move, work and live - while keeping every treatment firmly evidence-based, so the relief lasts.

  • One-on-one care. A full appointment with your physio - no double-booking, no rushing.
  • Evidence-based. Treatment grounded in current research, not guesswork.
  • A plan you'll follow. Simple home exercises that fit your real life and routine.
  • Part of St Matthew's. Physio that connects with classes, therapies and wellbeing under one roof.
